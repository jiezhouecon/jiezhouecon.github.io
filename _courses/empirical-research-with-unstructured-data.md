This course is about turning unstructured sources, such as text, images, audio and the traces people leave online, into measurements that can carry an empirical argument in economics. It covers how such data are collected and represented, the methods used to extract variables from them, and how to use those variables credibly in applied work, including what measurement error and modelling choices do to the estimates that follow.
